Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Fashion Analysta Analytics ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access and how it is used.

1. Data We Access

The App accesses the following data from TikTok's API on behalf of the authenticated account owner:

  • Video performance metrics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time)
  • Audience analytics (age, gender, location)
  • Traffic source data

This data is accessed solely for the account owner's personal use and is not collected, stored on external servers, or shared with any third party.

2. How We Use Data

Data retrieved through the App is used exclusively to display analytics to the authenticated account owner for the purpose of understanding content performance and informing future content decisions.

3. Data Retention

The App does not store TikTok data beyond the active session. No personal data is retained after the session ends.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's official API. TikTok's own privacy policy governs the collection and use of data on TikTok's platform. You can review TikTok's Privacy Policy at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Your Rights

As the sole user of this personal App, you may revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's account settings under "Manage App Permissions."

6. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the TikTok account @fashion.analysta.
